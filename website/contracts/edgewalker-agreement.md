# Edgewalker: 3-Month Container Agreement

**Between Kimberly Bryant of That Deeper Feeling and the Participant**

---

## 1. Nature of the Work

This is not therapy. I am not a licensed therapist, medical provider, or mental health professional.

This is sacred, intimate, and embodied reclamation work. It may include breathwork, guided visualization, somatic practices, sensual exploration, and space for emotional release and integration.

I commit to holding a safe, consensual, and grounded container for your exploration.

You understand that you are fully responsible for your own healing, choices, and integration. I walk beside you as a guide... offering presence, reflection, and tools to support you in reclaiming your voice, body, and power.

This is your reclamation.

---

## 2. Program Structure

This Edgewalker container includes:

- Three (3) 3-hour coaching sessions (one per month)
- Sessions may be held in person or via Zoom, based on mutual agreement
- One (1) integration support call (Zoom or phone) within one week following each session
- Guided breathwork and embodiment practices
- Optional somatic and energetic tools, including (but not limited to) mirror work, breathwork, and tantric-inspired body awareness practices
- Ongoing support via text or WhatsApp between sessions

Messaging support is available during business hours, with responses typically within 24 hours. This support is intended for integration and reflection, not continuous or real-time coaching.

All sessions and support calls are to be used within the 3-month container timeframe unless otherwise agreed upon in writing.

---

## 3. Consent, Boundaries & Scope

All work is consent-based, trauma-informed, and collaborative.

You agree to communicate your boundaries, needs, and limits clearly. You may pause or stop any exercise at any time.

All sessions are conducted fully clothed unless otherwise explicitly discussed, consented to, and agreed upon in advance. Any body-based or sensual practices are guided within clear, professional, and consent-centered boundaries.

This container is for personal exploration and development. It is not a space for explicit sexual services.

---

## 4. Nervous System Awareness & Emotional Regulation

This work is body-led and may activate your nervous system in different ways. You may experience sensations such as emotional release, vulnerability, arousal, discomfort, or shifts in awareness.

I will guide you with care, presence, and attunement, and offer tools to support grounding and regulation throughout our time together.

You are responsible for communicating what you're experiencing in your body and for honoring your own limits. You are always in choice, and you may pause, adjust, or stop at any time.

This work is not about pushing past your edges, but about building a deeper relationship with your body, your capacity, and your sense of safety.

---

## 5. Health & Medical Responsibility

You agree to disclose any relevant physical, mental, or emotional health conditions that may impact your participation in this work.

You understand that certain practices, including breathwork or somatic experiences, may not be suitable for all individuals, and you agree to take full responsibility for communicating any concerns.

If you are currently in therapy or under the care of a mental health professional, you are encouraged to inform your provider about your participation in this work.

---

## 6. Confidentiality & Recording

Everything shared within this container is held in strict confidence.

I will not share your identity or personal information without your explicit consent, except where required by law (such as imminent risk of harm to yourself or others).

You agree to respect the confidentiality and integrity of this container as well.

Sessions will not be recorded without the prior written consent of both parties. If recording is agreed upon, the recording is for your personal use only and may not be shared, published, or distributed.

---

## 7. Communication Boundaries

Text and WhatsApp support are available for integration between sessions.

This is not an on-demand or emergency support service. Responses are typically provided within 24 hours during business hours.

Boundaries around communication are part of maintaining a healthy and sustainable container for both of us.

---

## 8. Financial Agreement

The total investment for the Edgewalker 3-month container is agreed upon prior to signing.

Payment may be made in full or through a mutually agreed-upon payment plan.

If a payment plan is selected, payments must be made on time according to the agreed schedule. Failure to complete payments may result in a pause of services until payment is brought current.

All payments are non-refundable.

## 9. Cancellation & Rescheduling

A minimum of 48 hours' notice is required to reschedule a session or integration call.

Sessions or calls canceled within 48 hours or missed without notice will be forfeited.

In the case of a genuine emergency, please reach out directly. Rescheduling may be considered at my discretion on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 10. Term & Early Termination

This agreement is effective from the date of signing through the completion of the 3-month container, or until all included sessions and support calls have been used, whichever comes first.

Either party may terminate this agreement with written notice. If you choose to end the engagement early, payments already made are non-refundable, and any unused sessions or services will be forfeited.

I reserve the right to end this engagement if, in my professional judgment, continuing the work would not be in the best interest of either party. In such cases, a prorated refund for unused sessions may be offered at my discretion.
